- the brand of a food is currently in our database but it isn't displayed anywhere.  it should be displayed on entries in the food search view, and we should include it as an editable text entry when creating a custom food
- in the food search view, when toggled to "search the web", tapping on a food result should open that food into the create custom food screen rather than immediately logging it.  this is because sometimes the user might want to modify the values coming in from openfoodfacts before going straight to logging them
- in the food search view, when toggled to "my foods", foods should be sorted in order of most recently logged, with the most recently logged food at the top.  there is ONE exception to this: foods recently imported from openfoodfacts should appear above all others.  so the precedence is most recently imported > most recently logged
