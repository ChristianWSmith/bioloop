# BioLoop

A calorie and macro tracker that **learns your maintenance calories** from your logged food and bodyweight data. No cloud account required. No ads. Your data stays on your device.

[**Download for Android**](https://github.com/ChristianWSmith/bioloop/releases)

---

## What Makes It Different

Most calorie trackers ask you to guess your maintenance calories. BioLoop figures it out for you.

As you log food and weigh in, the app uses rolling linear regression on your paired data to estimate the calorie level at which your weight stays stable. The more you log, the more precise the estimate becomes — complete with a confidence interval so you know how much to trust it.

## Features

### Smart Maintenance Estimation
- Automatically calculates your maintenance calories from logged food + weight data
- Confidence interval shows estimate reliability
- "Perfect calibration" indicator when your weight is stable despite calorie variance
- Progress tracker shows how much data you've collected

### Food Logging
- **Search the web** — powered by the OpenFoodFacts database
- **Barcode scanner** — scan product barcodes for instant lookup
- **Custom foods** — create and save your own entries
- **Recipes** — build composite dishes from saved ingredients, log them as a single entry
- **Meal types** — organize entries as Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, or Snack
- **Flexible servings** — 11+ common units plus custom units, with smart quantity formatting

### Bodyweight Tracking
- Quick daily weigh-ins with date picker
- Edit or delete past entries
- Metric (kg) and imperial (lb) support

### Dashboard
- Calorie and macro rings showing daily progress
- Bodyweight sparkline with 1-month, 6-month, and all-time views
- Rate-of-change preview based on your calorie target

### Goal Setting
- Cut, maintain, or bulk with sensible defaults
- Protein target slider with bodyweight or height-based calculation
- Fat percentage slider with live preview
- Live rate-of-change estimates and warnings for aggressive targets

### Data Control
- Export food logs and bodyweight history as CSV
- Share or save files directly from the app
- Full data reset option in settings
- Customizable accent color (8 options)

## Privacy

BioLoop stores everything locally on your device. There is no account system, no cloud sync, and no telemetry. Your food logs, weight entries, and personal data never leave your phone.
